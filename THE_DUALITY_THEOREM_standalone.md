## Statement

Let S = F₃[s₀,…,s₅], Q = 3^v, m_Q = (s₀^Q,…,s₅^Q), R_Q := S/m_Q. For each perfect matching J of the six indices let I_J be its leg ideal and **π_J := ∏_{{a,b}∈J}(s_a+s_b)** its **leg cubic**; let E = (e₁,e₃,e₅) = ∩_J I_J (Odd Symmetric Theorem). Bars denote images in R_Q.

> **Theorem 1 (principal annihilators).** R_Q is Artinian Gorenstein, and for every matching J:  **ann_{R_Q}(Ī_J) = (π̄_J^{\,Q−1})** — principal, generated by the (Q−1)-st power of the leg cubic.

> **Theorem 2 (the dual bone).** For every v, the bone ∩_J(I_J+m_Q) = E + m_Q is **equivalent** to
> **(DUAL-v):** (m_Q : e₁) ∩ (m_Q : e₃) ∩ (m_Q : e₅) = Σ_J (π_J^{\,Q−1}) + m_Q.
> The inclusion ⊇ holds unconditionally at every v.

> **Theorem 3 (Frobenius self-similarity of the dual data).** With Q = 3Q′ and F(a) = a³:
> (i) e_k(s)³ = e_k(s³) — the elementary symmetric polynomials commute with Frobenius;
> (ii) **π_J^{\,Q−1} = F(π_J^{\,Q′−1}) · π_J²** — the level-v dual generator is the cube of the level-(v−1) generator times the fixed square;
> (iii) m_Q = m_{Q′}^{[3]}.

## Proofs

**Theorem 1.** R_Q is an Artinian complete intersection (six powers of the coordinates), hence Gorenstein with socle degree 6(Q−1); Macaulay duality makes ann an inclusion-reversing bijection on ideals with ann(A∩B) = ann A + ann B. Choose (L2) a frame y whose first three coordinates are J's leg forms, so R_Q ≅ F₃[y]/(y₁^Q,…,y₆^Q) and Ī_J = (y₁,y₂,y₃). Then x·y₁ = 0 iff x ∈ (y₁^{Q−1}), so ann((y₁,y₂,y₃)) = (y₁^{Q−1})∩(y₂^{Q−1})∩(y₃^{Q−1}) = ((y₁y₂y₃)^{Q−1}) = (π̄_J^{Q−1}). ∎

**Theorem 2.** ann(∩_J Ī_J) = Σ_J ann(Ī_J) = Σ_J(π̄_J^{Q−1}) by Theorem 1; ann(Ē) = ∩_k ann(ē_k) = ∩_k (m_Q:e_k)/m_Q. Since ann is a bijection, ∩Ī_J = Ē ⟺ their annihilators agree ⟺ (DUAL-v). The bone modulo m_Q *is* ∩Ī_J = Ē, giving the equivalence. **Free inclusion (two lines, all v):** by the Odd Symmetric Theorem e_k ∈ I_J *exactly*, so e_k = Σcᵢℓᵢ over J's forms, and each ℓᵢ·π_J^{Q−1} contains ℓᵢ^Q ∈ m_Q (L2); hence e_k·π_J^{Q−1} ∈ m_Q for all k, J, i.e. Σ(π^{Q−1}) + m_Q ⊆ the triple colon. ∎

**Theorem 3.** (i) Cubing is additive in char 3, so (Σ_T ∏ s)³ = Σ_T ∏ s³. (ii) Q−1 = 3(Q′−1) + 2, so π^{Q−1} = (π^{Q′−1})³·π². (iii) By definition of the generators. ∎
